# PunchOut Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 09/01/2026

PunchOut (“the App”) respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

PunchOut does not collect, store, transmit, or process personal data on external servers.

All data created in the App — including folders, punch lists, items, images, notes, and exported PDFs — is stored locally on your device.

We do not collect:
- Names
- Email addresses
- Location data
- Analytics data
- Usage tracking
- Device identifiers
- Construction project data
- Photos or media

---

## 2. On-Device Storage

All information you enter into PunchOut remains on your device.

We do not have access to your:
- Punch list data
- Images
- PDFs
- Notes
- Project information

If you delete the App, your data may be permanently removed depending on your device settings.

We are not responsible for data loss due to device damage, deletion, or uninstallation.

---

## 3. Payments

If you purchase a paid tier or subscription, payment processing is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play.

We do not:
- Collect payment information
- Store credit card data
- Access your billing details

Payment data is governed by the respective app marketplace’s privacy policy.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer user data because we do not collect or store it.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

PunchOut does not integrate with third-party APIs, analytics providers, advertising networks, or cloud services.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

PunchOut is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

## 7. Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, security depends on your device’s operating system and access controls.

We recommend using device-level protections such as passcodes or biometric locks.

---

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date.
